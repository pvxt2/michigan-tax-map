    Tax = SEV × total mills ÷ 1,000, using the state's total rates without special assessments. Taxable value is assumed equal to SEV. Zoomed out, counties and townships show the area-weighted average of the tax areas inside them; zoom in for each school district and village. Dashed outlines are villages. Transfer (“TR-”) districts are left out. A * marks areas where the state lists a second rate for the same district.
